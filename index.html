Step-by-Step Guide

How to Login to Your Total Defense Account

Logging into your Total Defense account is a straightforward process. Before you begin, make sure you have your registered email address and password ready. Follow these general steps to access your account:

1

Open Your Web Browser

Launch any modern web browser on your device — such as Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge, or Safari.

2

Navigate to the Total Defense Login Page

Go to the official Total Defense website and locate the "My Account" or "Login" link, typically found in the top-right corner of the homepage.

3

Enter Your Registered Email Address

Type the email address you used when creating your Total Defense account. Ensure there are no typos and that the correct email is entered.

4

Enter Your Password

Input your account password. Your password is case-sensitive, so double-check that Caps Lock is off and you're entering it correctly.

5

Click "Sign In" or "Login"

Once both fields are filled, click the "Sign In" button to access your Total Defense account dashboard.

6

Explore Your Dashboard

You are now logged in! From here you can view your protected devices, manage your subscription, download software, and configure your security settings.

🔐 Security Tip Always ensure you're on the official Total Defense website before entering your credentials. Look for the padlock icon in the address bar to confirm a secure HTTPS connection.
Multiple Devices

How to Login on Multiple Devices

Total Defense is designed to work seamlessly across a variety of devices and operating systems. Here's how you can log in on each platform:

Logging In on Windows PC

  1. Open your preferred browser (Chrome, Edge, or Firefox).
  2. Visit the official Total Defense website and click My Account in the top-right navigation.
  3. On the login page, enter your registered email address in the first field.
  4. Enter your password in the second field. Make sure Caps Lock is off.
  5. Click the Sign In button to access your dashboard.
  6. Alternatively, launch the Total Defense desktop application installed on your PC and sign in from within the app using the same credentials.
  7. Enable the "Keep me logged in" checkbox if you want to stay signed in on your personal, trusted computer.

Logging In on Mac

  1. Open Safari, Chrome, or Firefox on your Mac.
  2. Go to the official Total Defense website and click Login or My Account.
  3. Type your email address linked to your Total Defense subscription.
  4. Enter your account password carefully, as it is case-sensitive.
  5. Click Sign In. You will be redirected to your personal security dashboard.
  6. If you have the Total Defense application for Mac installed, you can also sign in directly through the app interface.
  7. Use macOS Keychain or your browser's password manager to save credentials securely for future logins.

Logging In on Android

  1. Download the Total Defense Mobile Security app from the Google Play Store if not already installed.
  2. Open the app and tap the Sign In button on the welcome screen.
  3. Enter your registered email address associated with your Total Defense account.
  4. Type your account password and tap Login.
  5. Alternatively, open Google Chrome or any mobile browser, visit the Total Defense website, and log in through the web portal.
  6. You may be prompted to complete an additional security verification step on first login from a new device.
  7. Enable biometric login (fingerprint or face unlock) within the app settings for faster future access.

Logging In on iPhone / iPad

  1. Download the Total Defense Mobile Security app from the Apple App Store.
  2. Open the app and tap Sign In on the main screen.
  3. Enter your email address and password in the respective fields.
  4. Tap Login to access your account.
  5. For browser-based access, open Safari or another browser, navigate to the Total Defense website, and tap My Account.
  6. Complete any two-step verification if prompted, for enhanced account security.
  7. Use Face ID or Touch ID to enable biometric authentication within the app for a faster, more secure login experience.
📌 Note on Multi-Device Login Your Total Defense account allows you to be signed in on multiple devices simultaneously based on your subscription plan. The number of devices you can protect depends on the plan you purchased.
Troubleshooting

Troubleshooting Login Issues

Encountering problems while trying to sign in? Here are the most common login issues and how to resolve them quickly:

I forgot my password. How do I reset it? ▼
On the login page, click the "Forgot Password?" link located below the password field. Enter your registered email address and click Submit. You will receive a password reset email within a few minutes. Open the email and click the reset link, then follow the prompts to create a new, strong password. If you don't see the email, check your spam or junk folder.
I'm seeing an "Invalid credentials" error. What should I do? ▼
This error typically means either the email or password you entered is incorrect. Double-check for typos in your email address. Make sure Caps Lock is off when typing your password. Try copying and pasting your password if you stored it in a password manager. If you're still unable to log in, use the "Forgot Password?" option to reset your credentials.
My account appears to be locked. How can I unlock it? ▼
Account lockouts typically occur after multiple failed login attempts as a security precaution. Wait approximately 15–30 minutes before trying again. If you believe your account has been locked incorrectly, use the password reset option to regain access. For persistent lockout issues, reaching out to Total Defense customer support is recommended.
I'm not receiving the password reset email. What should I check? ▼
First, verify you entered the correct registered email address. Check your spam, junk, or promotions folder as automated emails sometimes get filtered there. Ensure your inbox is not full. Also, add the Total Defense email domain to your safe senders list. Wait a few minutes and try requesting the reset email again. If none of these steps work, contact the Total Defense support team for manual assistance.
The login page is not loading. How do I fix this? ▼
Start by refreshing the page with Ctrl+R (Windows) or Cmd+R (Mac). Clear your browser's cache and cookies, then try again. Disable any browser extensions or ad-blockers that might interfere with the page loading. Try using a different web browser or try in Incognito/Private mode. Check your internet connection to ensure you are online. If the site appears to be down, try again after some time.
I've changed my email address. How do I update my account? ▼
Log into your Total Defense account using your current email and password. Navigate to your account settings or profile section. Look for an option to change or update your email address. Follow the prompts to verify your new email address. Once confirmed, you can use the new email address to log in going forward.
Two-factor authentication code is not working. What can I try? ▼
Make sure the verification code you entered was received recently — these codes typically expire within 5–10 minutes. Check your device clock and ensure it's synchronized correctly, as time discrepancies can cause code failures. Request a new code and enter it promptly. If you use an authenticator app, make sure the app's time is correctly synced. Contact support if you continue to experience issues with two-factor authentication.
⚠️ Security Reminder Never share your Total Defense login credentials with anyone. Total Defense representatives will never ask for your password via email, phone, or chat. Be cautious of phishing attempts that mimic the Total Defense login page.
